Andrew Fox is a New York City-born musician, producer, sound designer, and spatial audio specialist whose work spans recording studios, concert halls, theaters, museums, and multichannel installations.

As a songwriter and performer under the name VISUALS, Andrew released five albums, toured over twenty countries, and was signed to Domino Publishing, where he co-wrote Liam Gallagher's platinum-certified UK #1 single "Wall of Glass." His music has been placed in ABC's How to Get Away with Murder and the New York Times Magazine, and he has toured internationally alongside The Dave Harrington Group.

His practice has since expanded into spatial audio and immersive sound design. As Music and Audio Director for the Museum of Future Experiences (2019–2022), he curated and produced over twenty immersive works for a 13.1 speaker system and binaural VR, commissioning pieces from Robert Aiki Aubrey Lowe, Felicia Atkinson, and Maria Chavez. The New York Times called the results "succulent and otherworldly."

Andrew's Dolby Atmos mixing credits include Alanis Morissette's The Storm Before the Calm, Ela Minus & DJ Python's Corazón EP, and the Netflix film score for Fair Play. He has created multichannel ambisonic works at BRIC with Athena LaTocha and Maria Chavez, and designed sound for experimental performances at Performance Space New York and La Mama, including Mayfield Brooks' Whale Fall Wail Fail.

He runs Astral Sound, a Dolby Atmos-equipped recording studio and production house in Bushwick, Brooklyn. Whether mixing a record, designing sound for a stage production, or building an immersive audio environment from the ground up, Andrew brings the perspective of someone who has lived on both sides — as the artist in the room and the engineer behind the glass.

Sound Design & Installation

MoFE — Museum of Future Experiences

Williamsburg, Brooklyn

Curation, composition, sound design, and technical oversight for the Museum of Future Experiences (MoFE), a 16-seat immersive VR and spatial audio theater in Williamsburg, Brooklyn. The venue combined curated virtual reality films with a custom ambisonic speaker system that enveloped audiences in a 360-degree sphere of sound—one of the first spaces to integrate VR with an ambisonic playback system.

Mayfield Brooks: Whale Fall with Me

Wesleyan University Center for the Arts

Sound design and multichannel cello recording for mayfield brooks' Whale Fall with Me, a site-specific performance filmed aboard the historic 1885 Tall Ship Wavertree at the South Street Seaport Museum in New York. The project is the culminating chapter of brooks' Whale Fall trilogy, a multi-year exploration of whale life-death cycles as metaphors for decomposition, grief, and regeneration.

Kat Sotelo: A Devotion to Service

Abrons Arts Center

Original score and sound design for Kat Sotelo's A Devotion to Service, a new dance piece presented at Abrons Arts Center in September 2025. Select Immersive Installation and Live Sound

Athena LaTocha: In the Wake of…

BRIC

Installation and technical support for Athena LaTocha's In the Wake of…, an immersive 8-channel sound piece exhibited in the BRIC House Gallery from September 2021 through January 2022. The soundscape incorporated field recordings from nearby construction sites and a live microphone capturing the subway beneath the gallery.

Maria Chavez

Public Art Fund

Technical support for sound artist Maria Chavez for a multichannel performance and live film score. Chavez is a Lima-born, NYC-based abstract turntablist and sound artist whose improvised performances combine vinyl records with the electroacoustic properties of deteriorating media.

Ashley Systems

Ongoing logistical, A1, and installation support for Ashley Systems, an engineering firm specializing in systems design, technical production, and hi-fi audio sales and installation. Work spans fashion, art, and brand clients requiring high-end audio infrastructure.

C.A.V.E.

Ongoing logistical, A1, and installation support for C.A.V.E. (Compiled Audio Visual Experiment), a technical and creative production firm based in New York. Projects span fashion shows, art events, and brand activations requiring integrated audio, visual, and experiential production.

Ambient Church

Ongoing logistical and touring A1 support for Ambient Church, a nomadic experiential event series that transforms architecturally unique sacred spaces through immersive audio and site-specific light performances. The series has grown from its Brooklyn roots into a cross-country touring operation.

Performance Space New York

A1 for theatrical and music performance events at Performance Space New York (formerly P.S. 122), a cornerstone institution for experimental performance on Manhattan's Lower East Side. The venue commissions and presents genre-defying work across dance, theater, music, and interdisciplinary performance.
