About

Andrew Fox is a New York City-born musician, producer, sound designer, and spatial audio specialist whose work spans recording studios, concert halls, theaters, museums, and multichannel installations.

As a songwriter and performer under the name VISUALS, Andrew released five albums, toured over twenty countries, and was signed to Domino Publishing, where he co-wrote Liam Gallagher's platinum-certified UK #1 single "Wall of Glass." His music has been placed in ABC's How to Get Away with Murder and the New York Times Magazine, and he has toured internationally alongside The Dave Harrington Group.

His practice has since expanded into spatial audio and immersive sound design. As Music and Audio Director for the Museum of Future Experiences (2019–2022), he curated and produced over twenty immersive works for a 13.1 speaker system and binaural VR, commissioning pieces from Robert Aiki Aubrey Lowe, Felicia Atkinson, and Maria Chavez. The New York Times called the results "succulent and otherworldly."

Andrew's Dolby Atmos mixing credits include Alanis Morissette's The Storm Before the Calm, Ela Minus & DJ Python's Corazón EP, and the Netflix film score for Fair Play. He has created multichannel ambisonic works at BRIC with Athena LaTocha and Maria Chavez, and designed sound for experimental performances at Performance Space New York and La Mama, including Mayfield Brooks' Whale Fall Wail Fail.

He runs Astral Sound, a Dolby Atmos-equipped recording studio and production house in Bushwick, Brooklyn. Whether mixing a record, designing sound for a stage production, or building an immersive audio environment from the ground up, Andrew brings the perspective of someone who has lived on both sides — as the artist in the room and the engineer behind the glass.
